Paying for things
  • Both hotels take Visa, Mastercard and Amex. Outside the hotels, Amex and Discover are unreliable — lead with Visa or Mastercard.
  • Tell your card company you're going to Iceland before you leave, or expect declines.
  • Neither hotel has an ATM. There's one at the gift shop next door to Hotel Geysir.
  • An ATM withdrawal usually beats a currency exchange counter. Check your PIN works abroad and what your daily limit is.
  • Card is better than cash here. Iceland runs on cards for almost everything.
  • Call your guest room phone last — hotels charge a service fee per call on top of the rate. Use wi-fi and FaceTime or WhatsApp.
